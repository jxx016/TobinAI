The Problem: Landlords Are the Bottleneck

Every maintenance request requires the landlord to:

  1. See the message (often at inconvenient times - nights, weekends, vacations)
  2. Decide urgency and which vendor to call
  3. Text or call the vendor and wait for reply
  4. If no response, try backup vendors
  5. Update the tenant with vendor information
  6. Follow up to ensure completion

This takes 30-60 minutes per request. For landlords with 10+ units, that's 10-15 hours per month just coordinating maintenance.

Additional Pain Points:

  • Availability Stress: Late-night or weekend maintenance texts disrupt personal time
  • Mental Load: Remembering which vendor handles what, who's reliable, who has backup availability
  • Communication Gaps: Forgetting to follow up with vendors or update tenants
  • Lost Information: No centralized tracking of repair costs, vendor history, or response times
  • Tenant Frustration: Delays in response lead to complaints and poor reviews
  • Scaling Impossible: Can't grow portfolio without hiring property management (8-12% of rent)

Market Opportunity: According to the National Apartment Association, over 40% of rental units in the U.S. are owned by individuals or small entities—yet most property tech targets large firms. This is a massively underserved market.

Our Solution: The Landlord's Autopilot

Tobin AI handles maintenance requests from start to finish without landlord involvement. It's not just a chatbot—it's an intelligent orchestration system that actively coordinates between tenants, vendors, and landlords.

The Complete Automation Loop:

1️⃣
Tenant submits request via web form or text message
2️⃣
AI categorizes urgency (emergency vs. routine) and issue type (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, etc.)
3️⃣
AI contacts your designated vendor via SMS/email with full request details
4️⃣
Waits 15 minutes for vendor response (this is the key difference from basic chatbots)
5️⃣
If no response → Contacts backup vendor automatically
6️⃣
If backup fails → Escalates to landlord with full context and history
7️⃣
Updates tenant in real-time at each step ("Plumber contacted," "Joe's Plumbing accepted," "Arriving at 3pm")
8️⃣
Sends landlord a passive summary only after resolution (or if escalation needed)

What Makes This Different from a Chatbot:

  • ✅ Actually waits for vendor responses (not just "sends notification")
  • ✅ Fallback logic - tries backup vendors automatically
  • ✅ Two-way coordination - tenant ↔ AI ↔ vendor, landlord stays out of it
  • ✅ Closes the loop - tracks from request to resolution
  • ✅ Intelligent escalation - only involves landlord when truly necessary
